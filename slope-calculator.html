What This Slope Calculator Does

Enter the coordinates of two points, and this calculator finds the slope of the line between them, along with the line's full equation in slope-intercept form (y = mx + b).

How It Works

Slope (m) = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁) — the change in y (rise) divided by the change in x (run) between two points.

Once the slope is known, the y-intercept (b) can be found by substituting either point into y = mx + b and solving for b, giving the complete equation of the line.

Reading a Slope Value

A positive slope means the line rises from left to right; a negative slope means it falls. A slope of zero is a horizontal line, and a vertical line (where x₁ equals x₂) has an undefined slope, since division by zero is not possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a negative slope mean?

A negative slope means the line falls as you move from left to right — y decreases as x increases.

Why is the slope of a vertical line undefined?

Slope is calculated as change in y divided by change in x. On a vertical line, x never changes, which means the calculation involves dividing by zero — an operation that has no defined result.

How do I find the equation of a line from two points?

First calculate the slope using the slope formula, then substitute the slope and one of the points into y = mx + b and solve for b (the y-intercept). Together, m and b give the full equation of the line.
