What This Exponent Calculator Does

Enter a base and an exponent (including negative or fractional exponents) and this calculator returns the result instantly, along with a plain-language explanation of what the exponent means.

How It Works

Positive exponent: bn means b multiplied by itself n times.

Negative exponent: b-n equals 1 / bn, flipping the base into the denominator.

Fractional exponent: b1/n is the same as the n-th root of b (b1/2 is the square root, b1/3 is the cube root, and so on).

Zero exponent: any nonzero base raised to the power of 0 equals 1.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a negative exponent mean?

A negative exponent means take the reciprocal of the base raised to the positive version of that exponent. For example, 2⁻³ = 1/2³ = 1/8.

What does a fractional exponent mean?

A fractional exponent represents a root. b^(1/2) is the square root of b, and more generally b^(m/n) is the n-th root of b raised to the m-th power.

Why does any number to the power of 0 equal 1?

It follows from the pattern of dividing consecutive powers of the same base (b³/b²=b, b²/b¹=b, b¹/b⁰=b) — for the pattern to hold consistently, b⁰ must equal 1 for any nonzero b.
